# Ocean Gradient

A deep, professional blue theme conveying trust and expertise for technical content.

## Color Palette

- **Deep Blue**: `065A82` - Primary background, section headers
- **Teal**: `1C7293` - Secondary, cards, chart colors
- **Midnight**: `21295C` - Dark backgrounds, title slides
- **White**: `F5F5F5` - Light slide backgrounds, text

## Typography

- **Headers**: Calibri Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri Light

## Best Used For

Technology presentations, science research, data analytics, engineering reports, AI/ML showcases, academic conferences.
