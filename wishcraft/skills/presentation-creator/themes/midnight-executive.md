# Midnight Executive

A bold, confident theme with deep navy and gold accents for high-stakes presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Navy**: `1E2761` - Primary background, dark slides
- **Ice Blue**: `CADCFC` - Secondary, light slide backgrounds
- **Gold**: `F9A825` - Accent bars, highlights, stat numbers
- **White**: `FFFFFF` - Text on dark backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: Georgia Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Corporate presentations, financial reports, executive summaries, business proposals, investor pitches, board meetings.
