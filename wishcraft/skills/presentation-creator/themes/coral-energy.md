# Coral Energy

A vibrant, youthful theme with warm coral and gold for energetic, creative presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Coral**: `F96167` - Primary accent, section headers
- **Gold**: `F9E795` - Secondary, highlights, stat boxes
- **Navy**: `2F3C7E` - Dark backgrounds, title/conclusion slides
- **White**: `FFFFFF` - Body text backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: Arial Black
- **Body Text**: Arial

## Best Used For

Marketing campaigns, creative agencies, youth brands, social media decks, product launches, startup pitches.
