# Berry & Cream

A soft, sophisticated theme with rich berry tones for elegant, refined presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Berry**: `6D2E46` - Primary dark, title/conclusion slides
- **Dusty Rose**: `A26769` - Secondary, section headers, accents
- **Cream**: `ECE2D0` - Light backgrounds, card fills
- **Dark**: `3D3D3D` - Body text on light backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: Georgia Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Fashion presentations, beauty brands, wedding planning, boutique businesses, lifestyle content, interior design.
