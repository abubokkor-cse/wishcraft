# Teal Trust

A fresh, professional theme with cool teal tones that convey reliability and care.

## Color Palette

- **Teal**: `028090` - Primary, section headers, card accents
- **Seafoam**: `00A896` - Secondary, highlights, stat boxes
- **Mint**: `02C39A` - Accent, chart colors, emphasis
- **White**: `F7F7F7` - Light backgrounds, text areas

## Typography

- **Headers**: Cambria Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Healthcare presentations, consulting reports, NGO decks, education, wellness brands, trust-building proposals.
