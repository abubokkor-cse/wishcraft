# Cherry Bold

A high-contrast, commanding theme with striking red for bold, attention-grabbing presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Cherry**: `990011` - Primary accent, section headers, stat boxes
- **Off-White**: `FCF6F5` - Light backgrounds, content slides
- **Navy**: `2F3C7E` - Dark backgrounds, title/conclusion slides
- **Dark**: `333333` - Body text

## Typography

- **Headers**: Arial Black
- **Body Text**: Arial

## Best Used For

Startup pitches, product launches, urgent announcements, competitive analysis, bold marketing, TED-style talks.
