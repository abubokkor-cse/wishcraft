# Forest & Moss

A natural, grounded theme with earthy green tones for eco-conscious and health-focused content.

## Color Palette

- **Forest Green**: `2C5F2D` - Primary background, dark slides
- **Moss**: `97BC62` - Secondary, accent bars, highlights
- **Cream**: `F5F5F5` - Light slide backgrounds, text areas
- **Dark**: `333333` - Body text on light backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: Georgia Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Sustainability reports, environmental presentations, health & wellness, agriculture, nature photography, organic brands.
