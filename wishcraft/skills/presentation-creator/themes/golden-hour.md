# Golden Hour

A warm, inviting theme with rich amber and terracotta tones for approachable presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Amber**: `F4A900` - Primary accent, highlights, stat numbers
- **Terracotta**: `C1666B` - Secondary, section headers, cards
- **Warm Beige**: `D4B896` - Light backgrounds, content slides
- **Chocolate**: `4A403A` - Dark text, title slides

## Typography

- **Headers**: Georgia Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Restaurant presentations, hospitality brands, food industry, autumn campaigns, artisan products, cultural events.
