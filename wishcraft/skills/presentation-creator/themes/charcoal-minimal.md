# Charcoal Minimal

A clean, elegant grayscale theme for maximum versatility and sophisticated simplicity.

## Color Palette

- **Charcoal**: `36454F` - Primary dark color, title slides
- **Off-White**: `F2F2F2` - Light backgrounds, content slides
- **Black**: `212121` - Accent, bold text, dividers
- **White**: `FFFFFF` - Card backgrounds, clean areas

## Typography

- **Headers**: Arial Black
- **Body Text**: Arial

## Best Used For

Architecture portfolios, design showcases, luxury brands, minimal business proposals, photography, art exhibitions.
