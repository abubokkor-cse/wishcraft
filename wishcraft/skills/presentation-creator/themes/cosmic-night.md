# Cosmic Night

A dramatic, deep-space theme with rich purples for impactful, creative presentations.

## Color Palette

- **Deep Purple**: `2B1E3E` - Primary dark, title/conclusion slides
- **Cosmic Blue**: `4A4E8F` - Secondary, section headers, cards
- **Lavender**: `A490C2` - Accent, highlights, stat boxes
- **Silver**: `E6E6FA` - Light text, content backgrounds

## Typography

- **Headers**: Trebuchet MS Bold
- **Body Text**: Calibri

## Best Used For

Entertainment industry, gaming presentations, creative agencies, nightlife venues, luxury brands, space/science topics.
